Privacy

Last updated 1 July 2026

This is the privacy policy for gangabishwakarma.com.np, the personal website of Ganga (Merina) Bishwakarma. I have tried to keep it short and honest.

What this site collects

This is a simple static website. It has no login, no accounts, and no forms that store your details on the site itself. I do not run my own analytics or tracking, and I do not sell any information about you.

Services this site relies on

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare, which may process basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the pages and keep them secure. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a font is fetched. My Picks page loads product data from a published Google Sheet. These providers have their own privacy policies.

Links to other places

This site links out to Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Daraz, and other sites. When you follow a link or message me on WhatsApp, you are subject to that service's own privacy policy, not this one.

Google Forms

The studio page may link to a Google Form for enquiries. Anything you type into that form is submitted to Google and shared with me, so I can reply. Only share what you are comfortable sharing.

Affiliate links

Some product links, mainly on the My Picks page, are affiliate links. If you buy through them I may earn a small commission, at no extra cost to you. It does not change what you pay.

Cookies

This website does not set its own tracking cookies. Third party services linked above may set their own.

Contact

If you have any question about this policy, email me at contact@gangabishwakarma.com.np.
