Everyone else fixes the pieces. We design the whole.

Mery and Khem Studio is a creative studio in Damak, Nepal, the first of its kind to work across every creative and life field at one table. Brand, digital, content, strategy, wellness, and sustainability, joined up rather than scattered across a dozen vendors. We are the applied, for profit arm of the Hunate movement, the balance of Humans, Nature, and Technology.
