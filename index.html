01  ·  My Story

The Mystery & the Hustle

Hello, and welcome.

I am Merina / Ganga from Damak, Nepal — a young woman deeply curious about the wonders and mysteries of life. That curiosity has carried me through some of the most intense seasons a person can face.

I come from a lower-middle-class family where we have always had to work constantly just to keep moving forward. Since my father's hit-and-run accident, everything changed. He has been bedridden and in a coma for almost three years, requiring constant care, feeding, cleaning, and nursing. My mother and I work around the clock to sustain him. My younger brother is abroad, working incredibly hard to support our family. We carry loans and the daily weight — and yet, within all of that, we find peace, gratitude, and beauty in the life we have right now.

I hope this does not read as complaint or a search for sympathy. This is my truth. In this reality, my family and I have found peace, happiness, and reasons to wake up, smile, and look toward the future.

Choosing Kindness Over Complaint

I see many people comparing themselves to others or complaining about what they lack. As an empath, I feel these things deeply — but I try never to harm anyone or add to the noise. I stay humble, trusting that we each arrive where we are supposed to be when the time is right.

My father's accident taught me this. He was the kindest man I have ever known, and yet life still gave him — and all of us — this struggle. It showed me that life does not care who is "good" or "bad." Life simply happens. Perhaps that is what Maya means.

Rather than complaining, I would rather understand it. We can all still choose to be kind, loving, and caring in this beautiful, fleeting life.

From the Stage to the Bedside

In a different chapter, when my whole family was healthy and together, I was free to fly.

I learned dancing and fashion, won Purwanchal Miss Bishwakarma and was the 1st Runner-up of Miss Damak, choreographed performances, taught dance in schools, and judged pageants. I was also involved in The Joon Project — now the Hunate Movement — researching social systems and natural laws to help restructure failing institutions. Life felt limitless.

For the past three years, I have not left my father's bedside. I sometimes feel "late" in life, watching friends on social media get married, travel, build careers. But despite those moments of doubt, I do my best. I tell life: it is okay.

Life happens as it has to. All we must do is work — for ourselves, our family, our community, nature, and the future. Share what we have in excess. See beauty in all things. Ask for help when we need it. Stay positive. Life will give us what we need if we remain humble and grateful.

Growth Through Struggle

These years have also been years of immense growth.

I started Merry El's — Pinch of Style, an online 18K gold-plated jewelry store. I began creating content to share my journey honestly, and I received support from people around the world for my father's treatment. Because of that kindness, we were able to take him to Siliguri and Delhi for care — and I will always be grateful.

That kindness fuels me every day. It reminds me to become a better version of myself — someone capable of helping others when they need it. No one's pain is inherently bigger than another's; struggle is often a matter of perspective. Some take it as a reason for anger. I take it as a reason to grow.

A New Chapter

Right now, while grounded at home, I am learning yoga, meditation, MMA, swimming, sewing, weaving, and gardening. I want to share this journey honestly, and hopefully inspire someone out there.

I also started RAW TALK, a YouTube podcast with my dear friend Khem — a man of deep, multi-traditional knowledge spanning Ayurveda, permaculture, systems thinking, and ethical leadership. Together with Khem I now also co-run Mery & Khem, a brand, digital, and life studio in Damak that helps individuals, families, businesses, and organisations across Nepal and beyond.

Khem helped me find my purpose and overcome limiting mental patterns. I encourage anyone seeking a new direction in life to know him too — visit khemrajpradhan.com.np.

Brand. Digital. Life. All in One Place.

One studio. One team. One long relationship.

Together with Khem, I run Mery & Khem, a brand, digital, and life studio in Damak, Nepal.

Most people end up hiring one person for branding, another for social media, another for the website, another for content, another for business advice, another for legal, another for personal guidance. The pieces never talk to each other. The brand drifts. The story fragments. Everyone spends more time managing the vendors than doing the work. After three months, no one remembers what the original vision was.

We built Mery & Khem so this does not have to happen.

We work alongside individuals, families, businesses, and organisations who want their life, work, and presence to fit together, not be assembled piece by piece by people who do not know each other.

Some people come to us for branding. Some come because their business has plateaued. Some come for personal guidance, family decisions, or to think through the next chapter of their life. Many come for one thing and discover the work is connected to something deeper. We take all of it as one piece of work, because in real life it is one piece of work.

One studio. One team. One long relationship.

How We Work

Discovery First.

Strategy second. Design third. We do not pitch in the first meeting. We listen, understand the situation, and only then design a response that fits.

Six Months or Longer.

Real change is visible only from month four onwards. We would rather decline than promise outcomes we cannot deliver in three.

Through Our Network.

For licensed work (legal, accounting, therapy, architecture, engineering), we coordinate and a qualified specialist delivers under their own licence.
