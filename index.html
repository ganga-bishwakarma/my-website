Brand. Digital. Life. All in One Place.

One studio. One team. One long relationship.

Together with Khem, I run Mery & Khem, a brand, digital, and life studio in Damak, Nepal.

Most people end up hiring one person for branding, another for social media, another for the website, another for content, another for business advice, another for legal, another for personal guidance. The pieces never talk to each other. The brand drifts. The story fragments. Everyone spends more time managing the vendors than doing the work. After three months, no one remembers what the original vision was.

We built Mery & Khem so this does not have to happen.

We work alongside individuals, families, businesses, and organisations who want their life, work, and presence to fit together, not be assembled piece by piece by people who do not know each other.

Some come to us for branding. Some come because their business has plateaued. Some come for personal guidance, family decisions, or to think through the next chapter of their life. Many come for one thing and discover the work is connected to something deeper. We take all of it as one piece of work, because in real life it is one piece of work.

One studio. One team. One long relationship.

How We Work

Discovery First.

Strategy second. Design third. We do not pitch in the first meeting. We listen, understand the situation, and only then design a response that fits.

Six Months or Longer.

Real change is visible only from month four onwards. We would rather decline than promise outcomes we cannot deliver in three.

Through Our Network.

For licensed work (legal, accounting, therapy, architecture, engineering), we coordinate and a qualified specialist delivers under their own licence.

My Story

A Few Words About Me

Hello

I am Merina / Ganga from Damak, Nepal, a young woman deeply curious about the wonders and mysteries of life. I come from a lower-middle-class family where we have always had to work constantly just to keep things moving forward. Within all of it, my family and I have found peace, gratitude, and beauty in the life we have right now.

I hope this does not read as complaint or a search for sympathy. This is my truth. We have found reasons to wake up, smile, and look toward the future.
✦

The Journey

In a healthier chapter, I was free to fly. I learned dancing and fashion, won Purwanchal Miss Bishwakarma and was 1st Runner-up of Miss Damak, choreographed performances, taught dance, and judged pageants. Around the same time, I met Khem, and together we ran The Joon Project, which today is the Hunate Movement. We volunteered at orphanages, organised activities for local children, hosted art workshops, ran events for international volunteers, and researched how social systems could be restructured for a better future.

Then everything changed. Since my father's hit-and-run accident, I have not left his bedside in almost three years. He is bedridden and in a coma. My mother and I work around the clock to sustain him. My younger brother is abroad, supporting us. We carry loans, and we keep going.

I have learned, through this, that life does not care who is "good" or "bad." Life simply happens. Perhaps that is what Maya means. So rather than complaining, I choose to understand, and to stay kind, loving, and grateful in this beautiful, fleeting life.

✦

What I'm Building Now

While caring for my father, I started Mery El's, Pinch of Style, an online 18K gold-plated jewelry store. I began creating content honestly. The kindness of strangers around the world helped me take my father to Siliguri and Delhi for treatment, and I will always be grateful.

From there, I started RAW TALK, a podcast with Khem about life, work, systems, and the inner search. Recently, we co-founded Mery & Khem, a brand, digital, and life studio in Damak that helps individuals, families, businesses, and organisations across Nepal build one coherent life and brand instead of scattering their work across ten different vendors.

Khem helped me find my purpose and overcome limiting mental patterns. If something in you is also searching for a new direction, you can know him too at khemrajpradhan.com.np.

The Philosophical Foundation

The Hunate Movement

The Hunate Movement has been the quiet foundation behind everything Khem and I do. A philosophical research movement that began as The Joon Project, it questions the failing social system we have inherited and works to build something better, with three simple pillars: humans, nature, and technology, in balance.

The depth of this work lives on Khem's site. If something in it calls to you, learn more there, or join us directly.
